QR Studio Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. QR Studio is provided by Draga Inc.

What the app does

The initial offline release creates and scans QR codes and supported barcodes entirely on your device. Camera frames, chosen images, logos, QR contents, and CSV files are processed locally and are not uploaded to Draga Inc or third parties. This release contains no advertising SDK, analytics, tracking, account, or backend.

Permissions and files

Camera permission is requested only when you open live scanning. You can scan an image using Android's Photo Picker without camera permission. Android's document picker is used for CSV imports and file exports; broad storage access is not requested. When you choose Share, Android's share sheet gives your chosen recipient access to the specific shared file or text. Your chosen recipient may process it under its own policy.

Local history and security

No created or scanned content is saved automatically. When history is enabled, you can choose Save to history for an entry, including sensitive content after reviewing it. History is limited to 200 recent entries and is encrypted locally using a key in Android Keystore. It is excluded from cloud backups. You can delete individual entries or clear all history in the app. Disabling history prevents new saves; existing entries remain until you delete them. Uninstalling removes app-private history and settings. Files you explicitly export or share may remain outside the app and must be deleted where you saved them.

Advertising

The initial offline release does not display ads and does not include Google Mobile Ads or User Messaging Platform SDKs. If a future release enables advertising, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before publication.
