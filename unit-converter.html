Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Unit Converter is provided by Draga Inc. The app works offline and does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network services.

Favorites, saved conversion history, theme, and precision settings are stored locally on your device. Use “Delete all” on the History screen to remove saved history. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

The app does not request sensitive Android permissions. For privacy questions, contact Draga Inc through its Google Play developer contact information.
